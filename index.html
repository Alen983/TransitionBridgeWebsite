What it is

TransitionBridge is an Agentforce agent for case workers. It helps students with disabilities move from school support to adult services without losing accommodations and deadlines along the way.

Demo video Code on GitHub ↗

The problem

  1. 01School IEPs and accommodations live in one place
  2. 02After graduation, students face separate systems (VR, SSI, employers)
  3. 03Gaps and handoffs get missed until it’s too late

What it does

  • Finds students and reads Salesforce records
  • Surfaces transition gaps and upcoming deadlines
  • Drafts referrals for human review (never auto-sends)
  • Escalates SSI and benefits questions to counselors
  • Logs every action for audit
